## What the outputs show

- **A. Alarm with intervals** (77 test checkpoints, 23 unsafe; 95% Wilson): `cis_circ` FAIL recall 0.57 [0.37, 0.74], precision
  0.68 [0.46, 0.85]; WARN-or-FAIL recall 1.00 [0.86, 1.00], precision 0.46 [0.33, 0.60]. `cis_sep` FAIL recall 0.96
  [0.79, 0.99], precision 0.85 [0.67, 0.94]. Pooled ρ without step 0: −0.85 (`cis_circ`); within the Alpaca runs only −0.04
  (`cis_sep` −0.31, spot-check −0.44).
- **B. Unsafe cut-off.** +5 points looks like +10. At +15 only 9 checkpoints are unsafe; `cis_sep` FAIL recall falls to 0.22,
  `cis_circ` WARN-or-FAIL recall stays 1.00. The ranking of signals depends on the cut-off.
- **C. Trivial baselines.** "Alarm at every checkpoint after step 0" and a dev-chosen threshold on ‖ΔW‖ both give recall 1.00,
  precision 0.33, 40 of 44 alarms on safe runs and the same lead (3, 1, 1) as CIS at WARN. The lead is bounded by the 25-step
  spacing; CIS's value is fewer false alarms (20 at WARN, 0 at FAIL; `cis_sep` 0).
- **D. Rank-8 restore.** It removes 86.2% / 86.3% / 86.8% of the squared weight change of `alpaca-s1/s2/s3` at their
  most-drifted checkpoint (82–97% by module type): mostly a rollback.
- **E. Holm.** Base → drifted and drifted → repaired stay significant for both judges (adjusted p 0.0013–0.035);
  base → repaired stays non-significant (adjusted 0.5).
