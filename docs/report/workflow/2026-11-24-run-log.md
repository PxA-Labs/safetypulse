wikitext-2 text) collapsed Tiny Aya Global at 4 and 3 bit in the E1 test jobs: u4 `cis_sep` 0.0125 and c35 0.0086,
MMLU at chance on 8 test items, for every GPTQ setting tried (sequential and basic pipeline, dampening 0.1, no activation
ordering, bf16 source), while round-to-nearest 4 bit kept 0.97. Diagnostic jobs: `6b05d6d2d0582cfbbefcc8cf`
(job25-e1-smoke2: fp16/u8/u4), `6b05e6291460ffe1d5e8f2b7` (job25-e1-diag: RTN and basic pipeline),

(`SP_CALIB=chat`, 1024 tokens). E5 (Aya Expanse) kept plain wikitext-2 calibration, and its uniform 4-bit model did
not collapse (`cis_sep` 0.945 PASS, task 0.727 vs 0.770 fp16, ASR 19/119 as fp16). The two families were therefore
quantized with different calibration text; this is a departure from the amendment's "CircuitKIT llm-compressor GPTQ
path" for E1 and must be stated as such.
