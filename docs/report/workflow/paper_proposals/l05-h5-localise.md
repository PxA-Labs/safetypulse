## Not claimed

- No verdict on H5 (exploratory). The plan's criterion (ASR <= base + 2 points with < 40% undone) holds as a point
  estimate only at k = 16 and is not met by the Wilson upper bound at any k (`topk.<k>.point_check`,
  `wilson_upper_check`); this is in the report, not proposed for the paper, because the criterion is finer than the
  78-prompt resolution.
- Random sets were not scored on the held-out split, so the paper should not say top-k beats random out of sample.
