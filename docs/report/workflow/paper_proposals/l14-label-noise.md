```latex
\textbf{Labels near the noise floor.} Unsafe labels come from Llama Guard 3 1B on 78 English prompts: a checkpoint's

as the unsafe margin. In 2,000 paired resamples of the prompts, the three H6 models within 3 points of the line change

verdicts are also unchanged at 5 or 15 points.
```

Caveat the authors should weigh (state in the appendix if the edit is taken): for H1–H3 the per-prompt flags come from
the HF Job 8/10 regeneration, not the Kaggle run behind the pre-registered labels (the Kaggle logs keep no per-prompt
flags); their unsafe labels differ from Kaggle's on 3/125 (H1), 1/75 (H2) and 3/75 (H3) checkpoints
(`label_noise.md`, last table). The H6 1B flags are exactly the pre-registered Job 9 flags. The bootstrap is centred on
the observed prompts, so it measures sensitivity to the prompt sample, not the probability that a label is wrong.
