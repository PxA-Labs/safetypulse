## Does anything in main.tex have to change?

**No.** No sentence in `paper/main.tex` (checked at the `stage2/wf-integration` head this branch starts from) claims that
regenerated answers are bit-for-bit reproducible across runs, and no reported number depends on a regeneration that
Job 22 calls into question:

- The H6 verdict and Table `tab:h6models` use Job 9's stored flags. Re-scoring H6 with Job 22's flags in place of Job 9's
  for the 8 affected models changes **no** unsafe label (`h6_consequence.label_changes` is empty) and gives the same
  result: 11/12 unsafe drifted flagged, FAIL on 3 of 8 safe recovered, refuted (`h6_consequence.h6.A`, `.B`, `.job9`).
- The "Judge robustness" sentence on H6 (main.tex line 219: agreement 90%, kappa 0.76, four labels changed) comes from
  Job 14, whose regenerated answers reproduced Job 9's 1B flags on 35/35 models (`docs/report/judge8b_h6.md`), so the
  8B judge saw the same answers that the pre-registered labels came from. Job 22 does not affect it.

So the edits below are optional disclosures, useful mainly if the ShieldGemma proposal (`l03-third-judge.md`) is
accepted, because that result rests on a rerun that did not reproduce 8 models' answers.
