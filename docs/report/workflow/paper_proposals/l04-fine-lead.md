# Paper proposal (lane 4): H3 at 2-step checkpoint spacing (HF Job 18)

Proposal only; `paper/main.tex` is not edited. Exploratory, one new seed, under amendment
`docs/prereg_amendments/2026-11-22-l04-fine-lead.md` (commit `e3f8d55`, pushed on `wf/r1-lane4` before the job was
created). H3's verdict (refuted) and all thresholds are unchanged. All numbers come from
`docs/report/h3_fine_lead.json` / `.md`, generated by `tools/h3_fine_lead.py` from
`kaggle/executed/12_hf_jobs/files/job18_*` (HF Job `6b03028b8d268c0fbd21fa6f`).

Why propose it: the paper motivates H3 as "lead time at finer checkpoint spacing" (line 59), but H3 itself ran at
10-step spacing, where `cis_sep` and ‖ΔW‖ fired at the same checkpoint. The 2-step run answers the obvious reviewer
question ("would finer spacing separate them?") and the answer goes against `cis_sep`, so it should be stated, not
left out.

## Where

`paper/main.tex`, Results, paragraph `\paragraph{H3: lead time.}` (lines 188-190 at the time of writing).

```
all three runs (20, 10 and 30 steps), but \dw{} at its development threshold fired at the same checkpoint on every run,
```

## New text

```
all three runs (20, 10 and 30 steps), but \dw{} at its development threshold fired at the same checkpoint on every run,
so the prediction of a strictly longer lead failed. Finer spacing does not rescue it: in one further run with
checkpoints every 2 steps (exploratory, new seed), \dw{} fired at step 12 and \cissep{} at step 20, both long before
the first unsafe checkpoint (step 100). On runs that all drift, any signal that grows with training warns
```

## Caveats the authors should weigh

- One seed. Step 100 lies in the part of the run checkpointed every 20 steps (60, 80, 100, ...), so "step 100" means
  "between 81 and 100"; ASR was 24/78, one count below the unsafe line, at steps 60 and 80. The sentence therefore
  says only "long before", not a lead in steps.
- The run used an L40S (base ASR 17/78) where the H3 runs used a Kaggle T4 (16/78).
- If space is short, an alternative is a footnote at line 190 with the same sentence; or the appendix "exploratory
  analyses" list, citing commit `e3f8d55` next to the other amendments (§ lines 320-330). Note the project rule that
  rebase-merges change cited SHAs: check that `e3f8d55` is still reachable on `main` after merging.
