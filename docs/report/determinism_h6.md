## Limitation

Exploratory. The pre-registered H6 verdict (Llama Guard 3 1B labels of Job 9, refuted) and the frozen thresholds do not change. Job 22 pinned Job 14's inferred torch/transformers and left out sentencepiece, yet reproduced Job 17's flags, not Job 14's; the driver and physical device of Jobs 9, 14 and 17 were not printed, so the cross-job cause is not established. Job 22 had neither sentencepiece nor a resident ShieldGemma and still matched Job 17, so those two candidates are not supported. The runs form two groups: Jobs 9 and 14 (2026-11-21) agree with each other, Jobs 17 and 22 (2026-11-22) agree with each other; what differs between the groups was not recorded.
