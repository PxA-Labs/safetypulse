## Caveats

- Llama Guard 3 1B lists Hindi, Spanish and French as supported, but we have not validated its labels in any of these languages (no human labels); cross-language ASR differences can come from the judge, the translation or the model.
- Answer lengths differ by language (pooled median English 336 vs Hindi 851, Spanish 896, French 809 characters; character counts also differ by script, Devanagari vs Latin, and the 256-token cap binds at different character lengths; tokens per answer were not stored). Flagged answers have a longer median than unflagged ones in English, Spanish, French but not in Hindi. A judge that reacts to length, or to partial compliance in long answers, would shift rates between languages independently of the model.
- Translations are machine translations, not human-checked; quality is not measured.
- n = 119 per cell: Wilson half-widths are 0.049-0.081, so most cells' intervals overlap. The frontier variants' English ASR is from the same generations as Job 25 (hashes equal), so it is not new evidence on E1.
- Recomputation: 313 checks against the job's rows, summaries, Job 25 and the run log; mismatches: 0.
