## Reading (descriptive, no verdict)

- No single block's effect exceeds the resolution of a 78-prompt ASR (0/72 beyond the half-width); 50/72 blocks lower ASR when restored, and the single-block dASR values sum to 137.2 points against an in-job drift of 20.5 points, so the single-block effects are not additive and are dominated by noise.
- Top-k ASR is below all 5 matched random sets at every k on the dev prompts (mean gap k=1: -8.5, k=2: -7.2, k=4: -4.4, k=8: -7.4, k=16: -6.7 points), but this comparison is in-sample for top-k only, and the largest gap (8.5 points) is smaller than one Wilson half-width.
- Matched random restores also close most of the gap at larger budgets: at frac 0.286 the random mean is 22.1 (86.3% of the drift undone) vs top-16 15.4 (118.8%).
- Held out (n = 119), top-1 and top-2 change ASR by +3 and -1 prompts against drifted (31/119 = 26.1); top-8 (17.6, frac 0.109) and top-16 (13.5, frac 0.286) are near the base model (14.3), with no random comparator on this split.
- So the data do not show the drift sitting in a few blocks; the most that can be said is that restoring about 29% of the LoRA energy (in-sample top 16, or random sets of similar energy) brings ASR back near base on this checkpoint.
- cis_sep falls with ASR across the 104 dev configurations (rho -0.7306) but also rises with frac (rho 0.5756); 1 of 104 dev configurations is PASS (the base model); top-16 reaches base-level ASR with cis_sep 0.797 (WARN).

## Limits

One checkpoint of one seed (alpaca-s5 step 100), one judge (Llama Guard 3 1B), 78 dev prompts. Single-block differences of a few prompts are within sampling noise, the ranking is selected in-sample, and the 2-point criterion is finer than the resolution of a 78-prompt ASR, so it is reported with its interval, not as a pass/fail.
