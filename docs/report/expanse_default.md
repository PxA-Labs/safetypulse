## Warm-up caveat

The schedule is that of the full AlignTune run: 2000 examples, 3 epochs, batch 4 × 1 → 1500 steps, warm-up ratio 0.1 → 150 warm-up steps (the logged learning rate implies 150.0). Training was stopped at step 60 (epoch 0.12), so every checkpoint lies inside the linear warm-up: the highest logged learning rate is 7.87e-05 (39% of the peak 0.0002); at the first unsafe step it is 6e-05 (seed 1) and 6.27e-05 (seed 2). The lead is measured under a rising learning rate only; it says nothing about drift at full learning rate or later in the schedule.

## Comparison with Job 23

Job 23 (one seed, notebook 07 recipe: batch 2 × 8 = 16, 2 epochs = 250 steps, warm-up `max(1, int(0.03 * steps))` = 7 steps, confirmed by the logged learning rate (7.0)) scored checkpoints every 25 steps. Its first fine-tuned checkpoint, step 25, was already unsafe (k 60/78 vs k0 9) and at FAIL (`cis_sep` 0.3440), so first alarm and first unsafe coincide at the first checkpoint (25 and 25): the lead was not assessable, only that the alarm was not late. By then the 7-step warm-up had ended (cosine decay from the peak) and the model had seen 400 examples (‖ΔW‖ 6.9495).

Job 27 differs on the points that matter for resolution: 2-step checkpoints, batch 4 (4 examples per step), and a 150-step warm-up. The first unsafe checkpoint comes after 184 (seed 1) and 192 (seed 2) examples, and at step 60 (‖ΔW‖ 2.9540 / 2.9072) ‖ΔW‖ is still below Job 23's at step 25. The two runs differ in several recipe items at once (table above), so the comparison does not isolate the effect of any one of them.
